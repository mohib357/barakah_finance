### 📜 CORE ARCHITECTURAL & FINANCIAL LAWS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
1. **Single Source of Truth (Unified User Model):** A single `User` entity linked by foreign keys to multiple relational roles (`Member`, `Customer`, `Investor`, `Guarantor`, `Staff`, `Admin`). Unified login for all roles with 2FA/OTP support.
2. **Immutable Financial Ledger (No Hard Delete):** Under no circumstances can any financial transaction, receipt, payment, voucher, or ledger record be deleted. Erroneous entries must use linked `Reversal` or `Void` entries with mandatory audit reasons.
3. **Unit System & Valuation:** 1 Unit = ৳2,000. Fractional units supported (e.g., ৳5,000 = 2.5 Units). Live unit price is dynamically determined by dividing total active net assets by total issued units. Profit calculation is day-weighted and begins only when capital is actively deployed in business/products.
4. **Profit Distribution Engine:** Net Profit = Gross Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) − Direct Approved Operational Expenses.
   * Member/Investor Pool: 60%
   * Organization Fund: 35% (Operational reserve, staff salary, management compensation)
   * Charity Fund: 5% (Exclusive welfare allocation)
5. **Installment Calculation Engine:** Support Method A (Full Cost), Method B (Financed Amount - Default), and Method C (Custom). Installments must auto-round to whole numbers, and rounding residuals MUST be adjusted on the FINAL installment.
6. **Late Fee Policy:** Any late fee charged MUST be credited 100% to the `Charity Fund` and can never be counted as institutional revenue/profit.
7. **Print & Voucher Specifications:** Cash vouchers and debit receipts must be optimized for 5" × 7.5" paper cutting inside an A4 canvas (Margins: Top 0.3", Left 2", Right 2"). Unique sequence formatting: `M-XXXX` for members, `C-XXXX` for clients.
8. **Client-Side Image Optimization:** Canvas-based auto-cropper: Signature must export at `300×80 px (Max 100KB)` and Passport Photo at `570×450 px (Max 2MB)` with fixed ratio modal and face guide.
